Recall tells only half the story.

The highest AI recall is 66.50%, accompanied by a 49.53% false-positive rate. The highest displayed balanced accuracy is 77.10%, combining 57.33% recall with 3.13% false positives.

04 — DATA PROFILE600 AI IMAGES / V3

The collection,
in perspective.

A curated challenge set, not a representative sample of the entire internet. Recorded sources and image dimensions make its scope visible.

AI-image cohort only (600 images). Resolution compares raw and processed images by shorter-edge length; real photographs are excluded. Source counts describe recorded report websites, not necessarily original social platforms.
