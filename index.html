WildAIGC-Bench

AI images behind real news events and online claims.
A hard test for today’s detectors.

AI-generated and edited images from real news events and online claims, where fabricated content can mislead, enable scams or fuel public controversy. A continuously operating pipeline discovers and preliminarily annotates candidates; human verification and quality control determine inclusion. Photographic appearance and real circulation conditions make this a challenging test for image detectors.

Dataset on Hugging Face ↗GitHub ↗Paper on arXiv ↗

Dataset and paper publicly available · arXiv:2610.28087

BROAD COVERAGE

Across sources and events

Collection spans news websites, fact-checking sources, and related social media content, covering public figures, social affairs, disasters, and varied forms of image generation and editing.

TIMELY UPDATES

Following newly circulating images

Scheduled tasks continually collect, screen, and preliminarily annotate new candidates. Human-reviewed additions will enter subsequent releases, while each published evaluation set remains fixed.

HUMAN-VERIFIED LABELS

Evidence checked before inclusion

Human reviewers verify automatic annotations against image-specific evidence and perform quality control. Candidates with uncertain AI involvement or insufficient evidence are excluded.

RAW

The acquired online file.

Original collected bytes, including any existing comments, platform interfaces, identifiers, captions or generation marks. An acquired online file is not necessarily the generator’s earliest output.

PROCESSED

The region under examination.

Human-selected rectangular crops focus on the target image and remove surrounding comments, generation watermarks, platform identifiers or other cues where cropping permits. No inpainting, enhancement or resizing. Removing peripheral disclosures reduces direct textual clues that MLLMs could use to infer the label.

All 407 images were manually checked for crop suitability. Target regions were cropped where needed; otherwise the original image was retained. Crops are pixel-exact regions from raw, saved losslessly. Uncropped raw and processed files are byte-identical. Some cues can remain; processed does not mean completely free of leakage.

03 / CONSTRUCTION

Continuous discovery.
Human-verified releases.

Scheduled tasks already collect, screen, and preliminarily annotate candidates across news websites, fact-checking sources, and related social media content. Human reviewers verify annotations and perform quality control to decide final inclusion.

01 / AUTOMATIC

Discover, screen, annotate

Recurring collection covers public figures, social affairs, disasters, and other widely discussed events. Preliminary annotations use source descriptions, creator disclosures, watermark evidence, and fact-checking findings.

02 / HUMAN REVIEW

Verify labels and quality

Reviewers check image-specific AI evidence and decide admission. Uncertain candidates are excluded. No AI-image detectors are used for sample selection.

03 / CONTINUED RELEASES

Expand with new evidence

Each evaluation release stays fixed while collection continues. Future releases will broaden event and source coverage and add more AI images and real-image controls.

Planned real-image controls include original photographs underlying some AI recreations, enhancements, or edits, alongside other real images. These additions will support paired comparisons and false-positive evaluation.

04 / DATA PROFILE

A closer look at the collection.

The current release contains 407 verified AI-generated or edited images, with source pages spanning 78 website hostnames. It is a curated challenge set focused on news events and online claims.
