WildAIGC-Bench: A Hard Benchmark for AI-Generated Image Detection in Real-World Online Circulation

Evaluating AI-generated image detectors on evidence-linked visual content collected from real news events and social media circulation.

01 / OVERVIEW

From the web.
For real-world evaluation.

Images encountered online are often far removed from a generator’s original output. They may be reposted, reproduced by publishers, captured in screenshots, or photographed as part of a physical scene.

This project focuses on photographic-looking AI-generated and AI-edited images connected to identifiable events and documented online circulation. Each candidate is linked to its background and available evidence, then reviewed before inclusion in a frozen evaluation release.

“Hard” is the research focus. Small-scale preliminary tests suggest low recall for several detectors; full baseline results will be reported after annotation and acceptance review.

01

Observed circulation

Collect acquired online versions rather than recreating distribution effects with a synthetic compression recipe.

02

Evidence-linked samples

Connect each image to an event, source references, and an explicit assessment of evidence for generative involvement.

03

Human-reviewed labels

Keep collection judgments separate from final labels. Unresolved cases remain outside the accepted test set.

02 / DATASET CONSTRUCTION

A traceable path
from event to sample.

An evidence-driven collection workflow, with model assistance and human verification.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Find events and circulation claims through reporting, fact checks, and targeted search.

  2. 02

    Acquire

    Locate the relevant image and preserve the downloaded file without additional editing.

  3. 03

    Review

    Check image–claim correspondence, evidence strength, and photographic appearance.

  4. 04

    Deduplicate

    Check file hashes and visual similarity; group related works and events.

  5. 05

    Verify & freeze

    Accept reviewed labels, apply release screening, and freeze a versioned manifest.

What the benchmark measures

Detection performance on acquired image versions. Complete reposting and compression histories are not required and are not inferred from image appearance. Screenshots and publisher reproductions will be identified in the metadata.
